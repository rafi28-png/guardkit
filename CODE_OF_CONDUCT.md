# Code of Conduct

GuardKit is about protecting ordinary people from scams. That starts with how we treat each other here.

- Be kind and specific in reviews. Say what to change and why.
- Do not share anyone's real messages, numbers, or personal details in issues — anonymize first.
- Do not use GuardKit to build better scams, or to test how to evade detection.
- Disagreements are fine. Harassment, threats, and hate speech are not.

If someone breaks this, report it in an issue and a maintainer will act.
